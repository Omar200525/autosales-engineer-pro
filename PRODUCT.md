# Product

## Register

product

## Users

AutoSales Engineer Pro is used by IT sales engineers, solution consultants, procurement teams, and competition/demo evaluators who need to turn client requirements into a credible IT quote quickly. They are usually reviewing a brief, watching the AI pipeline reason through product choices, validating risk and budget fit, and exporting a quote-ready report.

## Product Purpose

The product converts text or image-based IT procurement briefs into structured requirements, catalog-backed solutions, reviewer-scored recommendations, and downloadable quote packages. Success means users can trust the generated bill of materials, understand why each item was selected, spot risk quickly, and move from brief to proposal with confidence.

## Brand Personality

Polished, intelligent, and impressive. The interface should feel like a premium AI-assisted sales engineering cockpit: confident enough for client-facing work, refined enough for a demo, and visually memorable without getting in the way of procurement tasks.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic Streamlit dashboards, plain admin tables, dull one-color SaaS templates, and interfaces that hide the AI process behind a single spinner. Do not sacrifice readability for spectacle. Avoid unclear status colors, inaccessible contrast, and decorative effects that make quote review slower.

## Design Principles

1. Make the AI pipeline visible and trustworthy: users should always know what is running, what changed, and where fallback logic was used.
2. Treat quote review as the core workflow: totals, risk, delivery, compatibility, and product evidence must be easy to scan.
3. Use polish to increase confidence: visual craft should make the system feel more reliable, not merely more decorated.
4. Keep professional density: show enough information for repeated procurement work without turning the interface into clutter.
5. Give status meaning beyond color: every pass, warning, failure, and active state should work for color-blind users and meet WCAG AA contrast.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA contrast for text and controls. Status indicators must use labels, icons, and shape in addition to color. Motion is allowed when it communicates pipeline state or feedback, but interactions must remain usable if the user prefers less motion.
